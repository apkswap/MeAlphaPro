# MeAlpha Pro

MeAlpha Pro is a local-first personal workspace for planning goals, managing tasks and habits, tracking focus sessions, and managing personal finances (accounts, transactions, assets, debts, and net worth). It includes evidence-based insights and a weekly review that highlights strengths, improvement areas, and practical next actions from activity recorded on the device.

## Product capabilities

- Goal, project, and task planning
- Habit tracking and focus sessions
- Personal finance, accounts, transactions, assets, liabilities, and net worth
- Weekly performance review with daily activity chart, strengths, weaknesses, next-week actions, and local report history
- Persian (RTL) and English (LTR) interface
- Jalali and Gregorian calendar support
- Windows desktop and Android builds
- Local-first data handling; external AI services are not required for the deterministic weekly review
